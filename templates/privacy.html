Privacy notice

This website is currently a case-study preview. Preview appointments are not sent to the clinic, and preview details are not stored in the database.

When services are connected

Cal.com will handle appointment scheduling. Supabase will provide patient sign-in and database storage protected by access rules. Contact details will be used to reply to your message.

Keep private details private

Please do not include medical history, identification numbers, or other sensitive information in the contact form. Discuss your health needs directly with your dentist.

Your account

Account sessions use private browser cookies. You can sign out on the account page. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not saved in the clinic's patient tables.

Questions and data requests

Once clinic contact details are confirmed, a contact for privacy questions and data requests will be added here. The clinic must review this preview notice before live use.
